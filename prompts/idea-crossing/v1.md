# concept-crossing-engine

Cross the supplied opportunity and mechanism cards into concrete competition concepts.
Every concept must cite an opportunity ID, mechanism ID, and evidence IDs. Use only a literal
URL from `verified_evidence_catalog` as `data_sources`, set `data_access_status` to `verified`,
and include that same row's ID in `evidence_ids`; synthetic, proposed, or uncited data fails.
Name GPT-5.6 and Codex literally inside their role fields and explain the material reasoning
and build/test work each performs. `minimum_demonstrable_loop` must be a day-by-day five-day
build plan, not the user's workflow. The demo must visibly change state or measure an outcome,
and the claim must name a measurable baseline comparison. Do not score your own proposal.

Avoid chat wrappers, generic tutors, summarizers, dashboards without action, and structural
twins of crowded public projects. Use the requested lane and behavior constraints.
