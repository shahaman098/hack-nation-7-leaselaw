Hi. I am competing solo in Hack Nation 7, Track 2, the RealPage rental housing law challenge.

If you manage rentals, the hard part is not reading one law. It is knowing which city, county, and state rules apply to one address, on one date, when the facts on file might be incomplete.

I built LeaseLaw Navigator. You enter an address and a date. The app shows what applies, what does not apply yet, and what we cannot decide without more building information. Every answer links back to the original law text. It is not legal advice.

In the demo I will walk through the live site and the checks we run on five hundred sample addresses.
