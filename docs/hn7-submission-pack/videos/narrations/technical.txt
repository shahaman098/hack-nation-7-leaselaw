Here is how LeaseLaw Navigator is built, and what was hard.

Module A extracts rules from the starter corpus. The challenge was trust, because models paraphrase. So we keep a rule only if its quote is copied exactly from the source, and How we decided shows the audit trail.

Module B matches addresses. Postal cities map to legal cities, like Boston neighborhoods, and San Ysidro to San Diego. Missing building facts were the next challenge. Instead of guessing, we answer need more information.

Module C tracks changes. The eval harness exits zero, with five of five change tests and ten of ten gold cases.

Limitations: one Hoboken rule is backed only by a link, and the hour sixteen ordinance test is not in this build.
