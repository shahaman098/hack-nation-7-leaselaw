# Finalist Evaluation Handbook

Use this document **only after shortlisting**. Do not feed it into ideation lanes.

## Purpose

Kill weak finalists. Do not generate ideas from this rubric.

## Hard-to-fake advantage (hackathon-relevant)

Does the concept demonstrate something competitors cannot reproduce with a polished mock-up and a prompt?

Examples:

- Real optimization result
- Live state transition
- Verified workflow completion
- Non-trivial simulation
- Specialist data pipeline
- Auditable decision trail
- Measurable before/after outcome

## Anti-chatbot test

Could a judge open ChatGPT and get essentially the same result? If yes, reject.

Strong systems usually include retrieval from changing/specialist data, deterministic computation, optimization/simulation, workflow execution, state tracking, tool use, visual transformation, continuous monitoring, or physical/edge interaction.

## Workflow collapse

Before: messy, slow, manual process  
During: system understands, retrieves, checks, acts  
After: completed draft, decision, booking, alert, or plan  

Ten tabs, three PDFs, two forms, and twenty minutes should become one verified action.

## Specific constrained users

Prefer sharp constraints over “everyone.” Example: final-year international students who require visa sponsorship — not “job seekers.”

## Data grounding and trust

Named sources, timestamps, provenance, confidence, human approval where decisions matter.

## Brutal elimination

Most generated ideas should die. Prefer one sharp concept and one backup.

## Technical proof sketch (allowed; architecture is not)

For the winner only, record:

1. Critical computation
2. Required data
3. Most dangerous dependency
4. Minimum demonstrable loop

No full architecture, roadmap, or implementation plan here.

## Official criteria first

Score finalists with **this competition’s weighted judging criteria**, not a universal invent-a-100 table.

Use hard gates, then pairwise comparison, and surface judge disagreement instead of fake precision.

## Forbidden ideation anchors

Do not reintroduce opportunity navigators, crisis action navigators, or ops-fatigue doctors as templates during ideation. Those structural twins are blacklisted in `corpora/crowded-archetypes/`.
