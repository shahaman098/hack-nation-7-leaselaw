# Hack-Nation 7th Global AI Hackathon — official tracks (HackOS, 3 Oct 2026)

Each team selects **exactly one** track. Submission: HackOS project + **Google Form** (both required).

**Deadline:** 4 Oct 2026, 14:00 Europe/London (15 min grace on uploads).

---

## 01 - ElevenLabs: The AI Apprentice

Build the AI Apprentice: an AI-powered voice agent that watches experts work, asks the right “why” questions at natural moments, and captures the hidden judgment, exceptions, and guardrails behind their decisions. Turn this knowledge into a structured Work Map and a voice tutor that can teach new hires how to perform the same work.

Sponsor: ElevenLabs

---

## 02 - Realpage: Rental Housing Law Navigator

Build the Rental Housing Law Navigator: an AI system that turns complex state and local housing laws into accurate, address-level answers. Given any apartment address, it should identify which rules apply today, explain them in plain language with citations, and show how pending or new laws would change the result.

Sponsor: RealPage

---

## 03 - Databricks: Agentic Scientific Discovery

Build an AI lab for the next breakthrough and make scientific discovery 10× faster. Use multiple AI agents to research, form hypotheses, plan and run experiments, learn from the results, and decide what experiment should happen next.

Sponsor: Databricks

## 05 - Buffalo Initiative x OpenAI: AI Atlas for the World’s Rare Diseases

Help accelerate the path toward treatment for the 5,000+ monogenic rare diseases that remain fragmented across research silos. Build an evidence-backed AI knowledge graph that connects diseases, genes, symptoms, studies and patient groups — and turns those connections into actionable next steps for patients and researchers.
