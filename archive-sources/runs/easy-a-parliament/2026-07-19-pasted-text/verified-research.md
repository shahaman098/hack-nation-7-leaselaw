## Facts
- [high] Hackathon runs July 17–19, 2026; submission deadline July 19 9:00 AM PT _(source: Schedule & Submission sections)_
- [high] Teams may have up to 4 members _(source: Hackathon Rules)_
- [high] All projects must be new work started during the hackathon _(source: Hackathon Rules)_

## Inference
- The hackathon strongly favors projects that integrate multiple models and demonstrate clever, non-obvious AI pipelines rather than shallow API wrappers — The Google DeepMind resource guide explicitly warns against 'incremental wrapper apps or basic prompt wrappers,' and the Effective Use of AI criterion rewards pushing tools beyond obvious use.
- Sequoia’s sponsorship signals interest in commercially viable, scalable AI-video tools; projects with clear market applicability and polished demos may have an edge in final judging — Sequoia is a venture capital firm; their hackathon sponsorships typically scout for high-potential startups and investable ideas.
- The requirement of fal session logs for Generative Media Track indicates that technical depth and creative prompt workflows will be scrutinized, not just final output — Submission requirements mandate session logs, which expose the actual tool usage and iteration strategy.
- Developer Track projects that reduce friction for creators (editing, consistency, real-time feedback) are likely to score high on User Value — The track explicitly targets tools that make AI video production faster, easier, or more powerful, aligning with real-world professional pain points.
