Winner

Casework Correction Receipt

A disputed case record goes from an ambiguous complaint in an inbox to a corrected workflow item with a visible receipt showing exactly what changed, why it was accepted, and which evidence supports the change.

User
MP constituency office staff handling resident casework escalations before a short parliamentary technology demo
Mechanism
m-001 verifiable-provenance-ledger adapted from supply-chain audit to resident casework correction events.
Action
The staffer applies a verified correction directly to the casework queue: the affected field changes, the next owner is notified, and a correction receipt records the before value, after value, supporting evidence hashes, verifier rationale, timestamp, and reviewer confirmation.
Demo
Load a fictional case marked 'standard priority' despite evidence of an imminent housing deadline. A judge submits a correction request, the AI cites the matching evidence note, the ledger records the before/after status change, and the queue immediately moves the case to urgent review with a QR-verifiable correction receipt.

Backup 1

Policy Consequence Lens

A vague proposal such as changing eligibility, review priority, or grant allocation rules becomes an interactive before/after impact view showing who gains, who loses, why the result changed, and where the model is uncertain.

User
Non-technical parliamentarians evaluating AI or crypto/blockchain proposals
Mechanism
m-000 counterfactual-policy-simulation applied to proposed civic technology or policy rules.
Action
The parliamentarian receives a one-page consequence brief with a plain-English verdict, assumption trace, uncertainty band, and a short list of records or cases that require human review before the policy claim should be trusted.
Demo
On pitch day, enter a natural-language rule change for a fictional parliamentary grant or casework queue, press simulate, and show the room how the AI turns it into predicates, reruns outcomes, highlights affected citizen groups, and produces an MP-readable consequence brief in under three minutes.

Backup 2

Bounded Next-Step Scheduler

A vague post-pitch intention becomes a concrete next action with owner, timebox, dependency checklist, and evidence gate visible on screen.

User
Civil servants running follow-up actions after hearing AI or crypto/blockchain proposals in a compressed parliamentary event
Mechanism
m-000 counterfactual-policy-simulation repurposed into constraint-aware action scheduling for post-pitch public-sector review.
Action
The official schedules the next bounded action: a specific 20-minute evidence review, owner, invite list, deadline, required input, and stop condition are written into a calendar-ready task rather than left as a generic follow-up note.
Demo
Paste a fictional pitch summary with three unresolved claims. The system identifies the riskiest unanswered claim, simulates which follow-up can fit before the next review point, and produces a calendar-ready 20-minute evidence check assigned to a named role with required documents and a clear stop condition.
