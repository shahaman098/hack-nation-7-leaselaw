## Inference
- Across tracks, trust, evidence, calibration, deterministic checks, and workflow completion are more strategically important than broad conversational UX. — Multiple tracks explicitly weight evidence, trust, safety, rules/math, calibrated confidence, and decision-support artifacts; shared constraints reject generic chatbot wrappers and dashboard-only projects.
- Sponsor tech is likely judged by materiality: it must perform core workflow work, not appear as decorative integration. — The brief states sponsor technology should be materially necessary and warns against sponsor technology used only as decoration.
- Projects that complete narrow, auditable workflows are safer than broad end-to-end platforms. — Several challenges specify one metro/program, one species, one mission track, or specific required modules, and emphasize demo legibility within about 60 seconds.
