# TxODDS World Cup Hackathon Brief

Superteam Earn World Cup hackathon powered by TxODDS / TxLINE.

Submission window: June 24, 2026 15:00 UTC to July 19, 2026 23:59 UTC.
Winner announcement: July 29, 2026 15:00 UTC.
Team size: maximum 3.
Required submission: working build, demo video up to 5 minutes, public repo, deployed website or functional API/devnet endpoint, brief technical documentation, TxLINE endpoint list, and API feedback.

About TxLINE:
TxLINE is a high-performance data layer for real-time sports data and consensus betting odds. It provides live World Cup scores, match events, and odds for all 104 matches using a normalized JSON schema. Updates are cryptographically signed and anchored on Solana. Hackathon builders receive free access until the submission deadline.

Common requirements:
- TxLINE data must be a primary live input.
- Products must be functional, not wireframes or pitch decks.
- Demo video quality matters heavily because live matches may not be active during judging.
- Notes warn participants to comply with gambling, gaming, financial, consumer protection, and securities laws.

Track 1: Prediction Markets and Settlement
Prize pool: 18,000 USDT. First: 12,000 USDT. Second: 4,000 USDT. Third: 2,000 USDT.
Observed submissions: 112.
Track focus: prediction platforms, consumer sportsbook interfaces, data dashboards, decentralized prediction markets, AMMs, escrow settlement engines, and parametric sports insurance or prop bets.
Judges value smooth TxLINE ingestion, compelling soccer or analytical UX, clean deterministic resolution and validation code, optional Merkle proof verification, and custom Solana settlement logic using TxLINE validation primitives.
Constraints: TxLINE credit token cannot be used for user wagering or transfers. User funds can be held in other assets such as USDC. Teams are encouraged to use TxLINE Merkle proofs and CPI into TxLINE validate_stat for trustless settlement.

Track 2: Consumer and Fan Experiences
Prize pool: 16,000 USDT. First: 10,000 USDT. Second: 4,000 USDT. Third: 2,000 USDT.
Observed submissions: 113.
Track focus: mainstream fan products using live scores, odds, and match events. Example ideas include group sweepstakes, AI pundit bots, and hi-lo stats games.
Judges value fan accessibility and polish, real-time responsiveness, original consumer interaction models, monetization path, and complete end-to-end execution.
Extra requirement: sign up through Solana.

Track 3: Trading Tools and Agents
Prize pool: 16,000 USDT. First: 10,000 USDT. Second: 4,000 USDT. Third: 2,000 USDT.
Observed submissions: 91.
Track focus: autonomous agents and automated tools that ingest TxLINE feeds and execute defined strategies. Example ideas include sharp movement detector, agent-vs-agent arena, and in-play market maker.
Judges value core feed ingestion, autonomous operation, deterministic and mathematically defensible strategy logic, novelty, and production readiness for trading teams or market operators.

Question to answer:
Recommend the single best idea most likely to win, and specify the track. Prioritize a buildable idea for a very short deadline with a strong demo video, clear TxLINE dependency, novelty versus listed examples, and lower legal/regulatory risk than direct betting.
