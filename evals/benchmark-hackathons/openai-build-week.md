# OpenAI Build Week 2026

Build a new application with GPT-5.6 and Codex. The technology must be material to the result,
and the submission must include a working project, public repository, and a short narrated demo.

## Tracks
- Apps for Life
- Work and Productivity
- Developer Tools
- Education

## Judging criteria
- Technological implementation (25%)
- Design (25%)
- Potential impact (25%)
- Quality of idea (25%)

Technological implementation is the first tie-breaker. Solo builders have five days.
