# Applied Research Seed Grant (fixture)

Submit a research proposal for a small seed grant supporting a six-month pilot study.

## Eligibility
- Applicant must be affiliated with an eligible research institution

## Required artifacts
- Four-page research proposal
- Work plan and milestones
- Budget and justification
- Risk and ethics statement where applicable

## Judging
- Research significance: high
- Methodological quality: high
- Feasibility within budget and six months: high
- Potential downstream impact: medium

## Notes
No live demo is required. No specific technology, platform, programming language, public repository, sponsor tool, or dataset is mandated. Data may be collected during the proposed study rather than existing at application time.
