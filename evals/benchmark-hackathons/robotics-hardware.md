# Autonomous Field Robotics Challenge (fixture)

Build and demonstrate a physical system that can inspect a marked test area and report detected hazards.

## Constraints
- Teams of 2–5
- 36-hour build and integration window
- Robot must operate untethered during the judged run
- Organizers provide the test arena; teams provide their own hardware
- Internet access is not guaranteed in the arena

## Required artifacts
- Physical prototype
- Safety checklist
- Live field demonstration

## Judging
- Task completion and reliability: 40%
- Safety and robustness: 25%
- Engineering originality: 20%
- Clarity of design explanation: 15%

## Notes
There is no required cloud provider, LLM, public dataset, software repository, or sponsor technology.
