# Early-Stage Startup Pitch Competition (fixture)

Present a new venture to an investor and operator panel. A working software product is optional; teams may present validated customer evidence, a service concept, a physical product, or an early prototype.

## Format
- Teams of 1–4
- Five-minute pitch plus five minutes of questions
- Slide deck required
- Prototype optional

## Judging
- Customer problem and evidence: 30%
- Market opportunity and business model: 25%
- Differentiation and defensibility: 20%
- Team execution credibility: 15%
- Communication clarity: 10%

## Notes
No required technology, dataset, code repository, cloud provider, or sponsor integration.
