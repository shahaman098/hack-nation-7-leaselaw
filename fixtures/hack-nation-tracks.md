# Hack-Nation 2026 — Official Tracks (fixture)

Each team selects **exactly one** track below. Requirements and demo rules apply only to the track you enter—not to the other tracks.

## AI for Public Services
Build tools that help civil servants deliver measurable outcomes—not chat wrappers.

## Sustainable Infrastructure
Hardware, energy, or logistics prototypes with a live constraint demonstration.

## Inclusive Access
Accessibility-first workflows for education, health, or civic participation.

## Open Innovation
Novel cross-domain mechanisms with verifiable data or simulation loops.

Judging weights: impact (35%), technical depth (30%), demo clarity (20%), originality (15%).
